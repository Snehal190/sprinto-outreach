The flow, step by step

1

Set the ICP

The Growth Lead keeps the Sprinto ICP or enters another one. A new ICP gets its own profile, written once by Gemini.

2

Find companies

Gemini searches the web live for companies that match, with intent signals such as a recent round or compliance hiring.

3

Score the fit

Each company is scored out of 100 against the rubric. 70 or more is qualified and appears in the Qualified tab.

4

Fill the gaps

If a fit question is still open, the scraper reads more of the company's website and the score is redone.

5

Find the contact

Hunter.io returns the people at the company; the one whose role matches the ICP is picked.

6

Draft the email

Gemini researches the person, picks two pain points and writes the email, using lessons from earlier edits.

7

Review and send

The Growth Lead edits and sends. Their changes and feedback become lessons for the next drafts.

8

Record it

Every step is saved in Neon with the company's stage, so the dashboard and the database agree.

Not in this map: what happens after the send (reading replies, proposing times, booking the call, follow-ups). That flow comes next.
